M1: ACCUMULATED MULTI-BIT WEIGHT FAULTS IN YOLOv8n
==============================================================================

Fault model   one fault = one bit flip at a uniformly random address in the
              3,151,904-value parameter store, at a uniformly random bit in
              [0,31]. Layer is NOT chosen: it follows from the address, so a
              layer is hit in proportion to its size. Faults are persistent:
              applied once, held across every image, then restored.
Corrupt       SDC + DUE, per image. An object vanished, was invented, or was
              relabeled (SDC), or NaN/Inf reached the output (DUE). Excludes
              'benign' (all objects found, right class, box moved slightly).
Null model    P(corrupt | N) = 1 - (1-p)^N, i.e. faults do not interact.
Dataset       coco-val2017-sub1024, 256 held-out images, 1,271 golden detections
Sampling      1,024 injections per N (independent draws, seeded SEED+N)

  any     = SDC + DUE, the measured corruption rate
  indep   = prediction of the null model at p = 3.20%
  lift    = any / indep. 1.00 means faults act independently.
  silent  = SDC / (SDC+DUE), the share of corruption a NaN check cannot see

RESULTS

  1. Faults do not interact. Lift is 1.00 +/- 0.06 with no trend across a 32x
     span in N, so the single-fault rate fully predicts multi-fault behavior.
     Single-fault characterization is sufficient for this model.

  2. Per-fault corruption rate p = 3.20%.
     Estimated by pooling the mid-N points, NOT from the N=1 measurement. At
     N=32 each injection exercises 32 faults, so mid-N aggregates 32x more
     fault events than N=1 and estimates p far more precisely: 3.20% +/- 0.23pp
     versus the direct N=1 estimate of 2.63% with 95% CI [1.74, 3.63].

  3. N50 = 21 accumulated faults to reach 50% corruption.

  4. Corruption is overwhelmingly silent. 97.5% undetectable at N=1, still
     84.1% at N=16 where the model is already wrong 41% of the time. DUE only
     becomes a meaningful signal past N~24, long after the model is unusable.
     A NaN check buys nothing in the regime that matters.
